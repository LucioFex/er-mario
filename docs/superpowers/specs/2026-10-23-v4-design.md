# ER Mario v4 — design (decisions approved 2026-10-23)

Goal: combat at Devil May Cry / God of War / Bayonetta level with fighting-game inputs, a new
Mario-themed sword, every RB shot its own move, Attack on Titan hook, Star Power rank reward, a
Mario Kart kart instead of Yoshi, a portable cannon, and a mod that never switches itself off.
Single player first; co-op must keep working (both PCs on the same build).

## Design pillar: DYNAMIC combat (the user's main goal, 2026-10-23)

Every new move and system is judged by whether it makes fights more dynamic: Mario keeps moving,
flows between enemies and between ground and air, and never waits on dead animation time.
- **Gap closers and repositioning everywhere**: each weapon has a lunge (back→forward + X), the
  dash cancels into blows (Dodge Offset), freeflow (B toward another enemy), the hook's rebound,
  the ice rocket punch, the Chomp rush.
- **Cancel windows**: after a blow lands, its recovery can be cut into dash, jump, swap strike or
  the next string step (shorter recovery on hit than on a miss). Only the hammer's heavy blows stay
  committed (its role).
- **Ground ↔ air in one string**: launchers (back + X, X held to follow, X + A, X,X,Y), the air
  strings hold Mario up, enemy step / air dash refresh, plunges back down into the next ground
  string.
- **Target flow**: lunges and finishers pick the next enemy when the current one falls or flies
  off; strings continue across enemies.
- **Mixing**: swap strikes and pair finishers keep a string alive across weapons; variety is what
  the style meter and Star Power reward.
- **Readable feedback** at that pace: hit-stop, camera punch, sparks, trails, soft sounds.
- Check for each part: in a normal fight Mario rarely stands still more than ~1 s; a typical
  string can go ground → air → ground with a closer and a swap in it; any landed blow can be
  dashed out of.

Research behind it (2026-10-23 session): DMC3/5 (Rebellion, Yamato, Nero), Bayonetta (pause
strings, Dodge Offset, Witch Time), Ninja Gaiden, Zelda (spin attack, sword beam at full health,
Flurry Rush, Helm Splitter), Smash (Marth tipper, directional aerials), Kirby sword, Tekken 8
(motion inputs, Heat), MK11 (Krushing Blows), SF6 (Drive), GoW (axe recall, runic, Spartan Rage),
Koei AoT 1/2 + AoTTG (ODM loop), Spider-Man/Titanfall/Odyssey traversal, adventure gadgets.

## Ground rules (from v3, still valid)

- Weapons and thrown things are real 3D meshes; 2D only for particles. Fluid animation:
  anticipation, follow-through, eased blends, squash and stretch.
- Sounds only from the SM64 ROM (or synthesised by the mod: the kart engine), soft when
  repeated (`sm64_play_sound_volume` ~0.1-0.3). Nothing third-party in the public repo.
- Not OP: enemies never melted or stunlocked (string fades, cooldowns, bosses resist), damage on
  bosses a few % per blow. Root-cause fixes, never per-enemy patches.
- Never drive the game / fight in the user's save without asking; the user tests and sends logs.
- Every part: `cargo test`, build + install (`build.ps1 -Dist C:\ER-Mario`, run through cmd; the
  Reforged copy `<ERR>\dll\offline` only once Reforged supports ER 2.7.2), log lines to verify,
  a review subagent on the diff, commit + push to the fork with README update, memory update,
  and a short "what to test" list for the user. Assets VERSION bump = the user launches twice.

## Part 0 — robustness, compass coin, light in the dark, perf instrumentation

### 0.1 Mario never switches off
Facts (2026-10-23 code read):
- `worker.rs`: a libsm64 call over `TIMEOUT` (3 s) sets `HUNG` for good → `lib.rs` sets
  `ENABLED = false` every frame ("Mario disabled until restart"). A Rust panic inside a worker job
  also kills the worker thread → the reply channel disconnects → treated as hung.
- `lib.rs` `m.no_ground > 30` (~3 s with no surfaces from Havok nor the raycast fallback): "Mario
  off, player returned to where Mario mode started" (RETURN_HOME to `m.home`, possibly far away).
  Seen in `er_mario.prev.log` after a world origin shift (+32 m Y) with collision not loaded.
- `frame()` panic → `ENABLED = false` and MARIO dropped; the `back` path re-enables it when
  grounded (already recovers, but logs nothing useful about the retry).

Design:
- Worker: no permanent HUNG. On timeout keep waiting for that same reply (the game stalls) and log
  every 5 s which call and how long; when it returns, carry on. The worker loop runs each job in
  `catch_unwind`; a panicking job returns None for that call only. If the thread is ever gone
  (channel disconnected), respawn it and rebuild Mario (new SM64 instance at his place) instead of
  disabling. Remove `HUNG`-driven `ENABLED = false`.
- No ground: hold Mario where he is on a temporary flat floor at his feet (like `FOLLOW_ENDED` /
  `flat_floor`), zero vertical velocity, keep querying every tick; after 15 s still nothing, put
  him on the last grounded spot (coins.rs `GROUND` spot, converted to physics) — never `home`,
  never Mario off. Log both.
- Frame panics: drop that frame, rebuild Mario next frame; repeated panics (>10 in 2 s) wait 1 s
  then retry, forever. Log each with a counter. `MARIO` lock: recover poisoning everywhere.

### 0.2 Last death's coin bag on the compass
Facts: `coins.rs` keeps the bag as (block id, place in block). `Spot::now` only resolves when the
player is in the **same block** → after respawning at a grace in another m60 tile it never shows.
Not persisted. The compass (`hud.rs` ~1621) hides it outside the ±55° span.
`BlockId` (fromsoftware-rs `cs/block_id.rs`): area bits 31..24, block 23..16, region 15..8,
index 7..0; overworld m60_XX_ZZ_00 tiles are 256 m (block = X tile, region = Z tile).

Design:
- Resolve across overworld tiles (area 60 and 61, index 0): world = local + tile offset. Axis
  signs self-calibrate the first time the player crosses between two adjacent tiles (compare the
  block-local position jump); store in the mod folder; log "coins: tile axes calibrated (...)".
- Out of the span: pinned to the strip's edge with an arrow (as the partner's edge arrow).
- ▲/▼ chevron when >10 m above/below; metres under it; pulses harder within 50 m.
- In the world: a soft golden light column over the bag (3D overlay, fades with distance).
- Persist per character (stats.rs style file, e.g. `er_mario_bag.txt`: name, block, x y z, coins).
- Bag in another area (dungeon vs overworld, other legacy map): grey coin with "?" at the
  compass centre-right (exists, direction unknown).

### 0.3 Light in dark places
- Real engine light: apply the **Lantern**'s SpEffect to the Tarnished (`ChrIns::apply_speffect`
  exists in fromsoftware-rs; find the Lantern goods row 2070's SpEffect in the regulation — verify;
  the user may also own/equip the real Lantern: don't double it). Check its light follows the
  posed skeleton (dummy poly on the waist); fallback: another light-carrying SpEffect/FXR.
- Plus a small Fire Flower flame mote by Mario's left hand (elemfx-style, subtle).
- Only in dark areas: by map area of the current block (caves m31, catacombs m30, tunnels m32,
  underground m12 Siofra/Ainsel/Deeproot, and the DLC equivalents — verify ids by logging the
  block id on entering such places) + ini overrides (`light_areas = ...`, `light = on/off`).
  Fade in/out over ~1 s.

### 0.4 Perf instrumentation (fixes come in Part 8)
Facts: in vanilla 2026-10-23 logs the frame task sums 300-600 ms per 2 s (15-30% of wall time)
even while exploring, with almost nothing inside it instrumented ("SM64 tick" small); the input
task's worst run is 15-20 ms every report (suspicious: lock contention or `bossride::pre_behavior`
/ `game_menu_job`?). Add `perf` spans for every sizeable block in `frame()` (combat target scan,
juggle, swing/stance watch, colossus, element/status, bossride, coop, coins, lakitu, hud data,
moves, fireball, effects building) and inside `input_task`; widen the report to list the top 5
parts by total. No behaviour change.

## Part 1 — data-driven move engine (base for parts 2-3)

Why: every new move is today a hand-written C action (`er_moves.c`, 3433 lines). 2-3x the moves
needs moves as data, authored and tested in Rust.

- `src/script.rs`: a `Move` = ticks + keyframes (eased curves) for: SM64 animation id and a
  **remapped animation time** (slow wind-up, fast strike, eased follow-through), body twist / pitch
  / roll, forward speed (+ the aimed lunge), vertical speed or "keep air", squash/dip, weapon pose
  (yaw/pitch/roll around his facing, scale), hand IK weight; hit windows (shape: weapon sweep, arc,
  around, front box, ring; radius; `Attack`; once per target or every N ticks; fx); cancel windows
  (which inputs may branch out, from which tick); flags (locked, invulnerable, air, ends in air).
- libsm64: `ACT_ER_SCRIPT` (ground) and `ACT_ER_SCRIPT_AIR` apply a per-tick `ErScriptFrame` set by
  Rust right before each SM64 tick (export `sm64_er_script(id, frame)`), with SM64's own ground /
  air steps (walls, ledges, falling off → script's air branch or freefall). Hurt/knockback still
  interrupts (action changes → script ends).
- Hits come straight from the script windows into `combat.rs` (`Reach::Sweep` generalised to the
  held weapon's segment: hammer head or sword blade).
- **Arm IK** (two-bone, Rust, on SM64 parts upper arm / forearm / hand, right and optionally left
  for two-handed blows): the hand follows the weapon's grip path; weight eased in/out; elbow pole
  out and down. Also usable by the hammer later.
- `src/combo.rs` input grammar (unit-tested): stick history relative to the target enemy when one
  is near in front (else Mario's facing) — back→forward (+ button within 8 ticks), forward, back;
  hold/release with charge time; pauses (beat windows); mash counting; chords (X+A exists);
  **Dodge Offset**: X held through a dash keeps the string's step for the next X.
- Existing moves stay as they are; nothing regresses.

## Part 5 — Attack on Titan hook

Problem: pole swing orbits the enemy centre with no goal (big: "spins pointlessly"; medium:
"swings to the side and does nothing"); hanging/climbing uncomfortable.
- **Anchor to a bone**, not the body: RT on a big enemy anchors the bone nearest the screen
  centre (nape/neck, head, arm, leg, tail; canonical bones as colossus.rs); right-stick flick
  changes bone; ring on it; camera frames Mario + that bone.
- Loop: left stick held = short orbit around the anchor (≤ ~90°/s, max 3 s, rope through terrain
  drops it); stick released = **reel in** 12 → 30 m/s; B = gas burst (+40%, 2 charges, refill on a
  hit or landing); **X in the last ~0.25 s = strike** (sword: AoT spin slash; other weapons their
  heavy), no X = glancing hit at half. Damage = base × clamp(speed / 20 m/s, 0.3, 1.8); nape/head
  from behind ×1.5 + heavy stance ("NAPE!"); per-hit cap on bosses; speed tiers readable (rope /
  trail colour, speed lines, hit pitch; "tink" below the lowest). After any hit: hit-stop, rebound
  up and back 6-8 m, A re-anchors.
- Medium enemies: no swing. RT tap = zip-kick + bounce up into the air string; RT held = vault
  over to land behind it (1 s backstab bonus).
- **Climbing only on really huge enemies** (raise colossus.rs TALL 3.6 m / WIDE 1.6 m to ~7-8 m /
  ~3 m; tune with the user), A while reeled against the body = grab. Remove the pole swing.
- Rope pendulum on scenery stays; attach ahead-and-above in a cone, not only straight up.

## Decisions (implementation)

- **Part 0, no ground (2026-10-23 review):** `havok_surfaces` returns nothing whenever no triangle is
  near (a 4 m box and a 40 m column), so a jump off a cliff over ~40 m or a wing-cap flight high up
  counted as "no ground" and the first hold design froze Mario in mid-air. Now a long game ray
  (600 m) tells a drop (catch floor where the ray hit, nothing counted) from a missing map (held
  after 1 s, NO_GROUND_HOLD). The held floor isn't "ground" for the last-ground spot (the 15 s
  fallback warped him onto himself).
- **Part 0, light:** an engine SpEffect light can't fade; the ~1 s fade is the hand flame's only.
- **Part 0, rebuilds:** a rebuild keeps the loadout and SM64 health; the Tarnished's gravity is
  given back (created again only over ground, a rebuild in mid-air waited forever with gravity off).
  A downed co-op player rebuilt comes back up (rare; not handled).
- **Part 0, tiles:** a crossing with no jump while both places are inside a tile's square is not
