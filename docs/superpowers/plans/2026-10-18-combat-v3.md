# Combat v3 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** executed inline by one agent (the files overlap heavily: lib.rs,
> combat.rs, moves.rs, er_moves.c), with a review subagent per batch. Steps use checkbox syntax.

**Goal:** implement the approved spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-combat-v3-design.md`
(sections A-H) in 7 batches plus the game-over trim, each built, installed and reviewed.

**Architecture:** Rust DLL (`src/`) drives libsm64 (C, `libsm64/src/decomp/game/`). New moves are
libsm64 actions (`er_moves.c`, ids in `moves.rs`), started from Rust with `Request::Action` before
the SM64 tick; their hits come back through `sm64_er_get_move` (`ErHit` -> `moves::Hit` ->
`combat::combo_hits`). Damage = share of max HP (`combat::Attack::percent`, boss factor, string
fade). Thrown/placed things are new gear parts posed every tick (`engine_mario::PartPose`),
3D meshes built at asset time (`assets/gear.rs`). Effects in `impact.rs`/`hud.rs`, style in
`style.rs`, co-op in `coop.rs`.

**Tech Stack:** Rust (cargo, x86_64-pc-windows-msvc), clang-cl for libsm64, glam, fromsoftware-rs
bindings (`eldenring::cs`), XInput.

## Global Constraints

- Real 3D for weapons/items/thrown things; 2D only for particles.
- Sounds only from the SM64 ROM; repeated cues soft (`sm64_play_sound_volume` 0.09-0.3).
- No melting/stunlock: per-target cooldowns, string fade, bosses immune to cheap tricks, launches
  never kill by themselves; enemy damage unchanged.
- Vanilla ER, solo Reforged and Seamless co-op keep working; new attacks are appended at the END
  of `combat::CODES` (wire codes) and the pose packet VERSION is bumped when its layout changes.
- Every batch: `cmd /c "powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File C:\ER-Mario-src\build.ps1 -Dist C:\ER-Mario"`,
  copy `C:\ER-Mario\er_mario.dll` to `C:\ERR - Elden Ring Reforged\ERRv2.3.6.3\dll\offline`,
  `cargo test --release --lib` (scratchpad test.ps1 env), log lines to verify, review subagent,
  fix findings, commit.
- Never move Mario / fight in the user's save; game may be opened for menus/startup logs only.
- Balance notes per batch go into the spec's section G log.

### Task 0 (H): shorter game over

**Files:** `src/gameover.rs`

### Batch 1 (C9-11, F19-20): timing + co-op touches

#### Task 1.1: Mikiri stomp (A just before a blow lands)

**Files:** create `src/timing.rs`; modify `src/moves.rs` (A edge, Request), `src/lib.rs` (call),
`libsm64/.../er_moves.c` (ACT_ER_MIKIRI 0x0080089A? -> next free airborne id, `sm64_er_set_target`),
`src/combat.rs` (`Attack::Mikiri`, appended to CODES).

- Detection = dodge.rs's: hostile within 3.2 m facing Mario, attack anim 3000-3999 with
  play_time in [0.15, 0.75] s (the blow is coming; earlier = plain jump), Mario grounded and free.
- Move: 2-tick crouch anticipation, somersault leap onto the attacker's head (ballistic to its
  top, invulnerable while rising), stomp (ER_HIT_MIKIRI), bounce up 38 u/t into a free fall
  (air combo possible). Enemy: Attack::Mikiri 12% (boss bonus 1.5), poise of GroundPound,
  `swing::add_stance` posture on bosses (toward the tail grab), 0.45 s witch time, "MIKIRI!" pop.
- Limits: cooldown 2.5 s; not on huge bodies (> 6 m tall: bounce off the knee instead = plain
  Stomp); not while Mega/Yoshi/grabbed/hooked; not in co-op guest on bosses (host decides HP).
- Log: `timing: mikiri on cXXXX (anim N, t=0.42 s)`, `timing: A early/late (t=...)` for the
  first 20 misses so the window can be tuned from the log.

#### Task 1.2: Action command ("Nice!")

**Files:** `src/timing.rs`, `src/combat.rs` (hook in `deal` for melee hits), `src/hud.rs` (text pop),
`src/style.rs` (bonus points).

- A melee hit landing (not ranged, not light) opens a window of ticks [-2, +2] around it; an X
  press inside it, with no other X in the 8 ticks before (no mashing), is "Nice!": the blow takes
  an extra 30% of its share (`take_pct`), +style, a soft star chime (0.12) and a yellow
  "NICE!" pop. At most one per 10 ticks; a 3rd Nice in a row = "GREAT!" (x1.5 instead of x1.3).
- Log: `timing: nice (x1.3) on Punch, streak 2`.

#### Task 1.3: Dash Bonk (X at the end of the dash)

**Files:** `src/moves.rs`, `er_moves.c` (ACT_ER_BONK ground action), `src/combat.rs` (`Attack::Bonk`).

- X in the dash's last 5 ticks or up to 4 ticks after it ends = Bonk: a lunging headbutt/
  shoulder thrust (MARIO_ANIM_DIVE first frames -> landing roll), forward 46 u/t decaying, hit in
  front (Reach::Front 190). 18% , poise of Stomp, cooldown = dash's. Earlier X keeps today's
  behaviour (cancel into the punch).
- Log: `moves: dash bonk`.

#### Task 1.4: Bros. Jump

**Files:** `src/coop.rs` (partner's head position API), `src/lib.rs` (check before the tick).

- Mario falling (vy < 0), feet within 0.45 m horizontally of the partner's head and 0.4 m above
  to 0.25 m below it -> velocity up 62 u/t (triple-jump height) + SOUND_ACTION_BOUNCE_OFF_OBJECT +
  ring. Once per 0.6 s. Local only (the partner just sees it in the pose).
- Log: `coop: Bros. Jump off the partner's head`.

#### Task 1.5: Partner HUD

**Files:** `src/coop.rs` (packet VERSION 2: + health u16 + character u8; decode accepts only 2),
`src/hud.rs` (small panel bottom-left: SM64 head icon (Mario/Luigi tint), mini power pie with the
partner's wedges, off-screen arrow on the screen edge with distance in m).

- Hidden alone / no partner pose for 1 s. Log once: `coop: partner HUD on (Luigi, 8 wedges)`.

- [ ] Steps per task: implement, `cargo test`, build, install, check startup log, commit.
- [ ] Batch review subagent; fix findings; balance notes into the spec.

### Batch 2 (A1-2): budget check, banana, shell

#### Task 2.1: vertex budget / second piece
- Measure the Vagabond legs and gauntlets pieces (lg_m_1280 / am_m_1280) vertex & index buffers
  (log at asset build). Put the trap kit meshes (banana, shell, Bob-omb, spring, + D-pad power-up's
  mesh) on the piece with room, skinned to unused skeleton bones (no bone above Mario's parts,
  no calves/feet). New parts after CHAIN (PARTS grows); the partner's copy too (coop pose packet
  carries PARTS: VERSION bump already in 1.5 -> same build both sides).
- Assets VERSION +1 (two launches after rebuild).

#### Task 2.2: traps.rs core + banana (LT+X)
- LT held, standing/crouching (not running) + X: Mario places/throws a banana 1.2 m ahead
  (ACT_ER_PLACE: crouch-and-drop anim). Max 2 on the floor, 10 s cooldown per banana, 45 s life.
- Commons stepping on it (feet within 0.6 m): `knock::slip` = short semi-ragdoll fall (2 s down)
  + 8% damage + "Slip!"; mediums (boss_class false but big) stagger (bullet with Sweep poise);
  bosses ignore it. Banana consumed.

#### Task 2.3: green shell (LT+Y) / red shell (LT+Y held)
- Kicked along the floor at 32 u/s... (SM64 floor step: sticks to slopes, reflects off walls
  with Havok rays), bounces off enemies, 4 hits then gone (red: 2 bounces, homes on the nearest
  enemy, turn rate 3 rad/s). Each hit 9% + knockdown on commons (knock::trip), shell bounces back.
  "Carom" skillshot hook (3 enemies by one shell). Cooldown 8 s.

### Batch 4 (B5-7)
- Home run: X while a small enemy is pulled (juggle Phase::Pulled) -> bat swing (hammer if out,
  else fist) sends it flying along the camera forward (knock::launch strong), bowling others
  (impact on contact 12%). cd per enemy.
- Tether: hook latched on enemy A, RT on enemy B within 15 m -> both pulled together, collide
  at the midpoint: both 10% + stagger + "Tether!" ; bosses can't be the pulled side.
- Pole swing: RT held on an enemy hooked (not pulled: big or holding) -> Mario orbits it at 2.5 m
  (ACT_ER_POLE_SWING), release = flying kick along the tangent with the momentum (Attack::SwingKick
  20%).

### Batch 5 (D12-14)
- Directional variants: stick back (relative to Mario's facing, > 0.6) + X = launcher for every
  weapon (fists uppercut-lite, hammer = existing launcher, Chomp bite, boomerang upper); stick
  forward + Y = gap closer (fists: flying kick dash, hammer: leaping smash, Chomp: lunge bite,
  boomerang: dash throw).
- MAX finishers: style streak of 12 hits without taking damage -> weapon glows (impact glow),
  next finisher of the weapon is MAX (Chomp Cyclone MAX fling, hammer cross quake, triple big
  boomerang, fists flurry+uppercut). Hit = streak lost.
- Fluidity pass: audit every action's anim switches (set_mario_animation without blend ->
  use anim accel/frame offsets, er_lerp on angles), anticipation/follow-through frames, squash &
  stretch, hit freeze + camera punch on finishers, per-weapon trail colours, enemy flash.
  Chomp cyclone Blades-of-Exile: wind-up, wide arcs with momentum, fire ribbon along the chain,
  soft clinks, recovery, ball trailing.

### Batch 6 (E15-16)
- Skillshots (style.rs): named bonuses ("Slip!", "Strike!", "Home Run!", "Carom!", "Tether!",
  "Mikiri!", "Wall Splat!", "Air Raid!") with points and a gamified popup.
- Freeflow: B pushed toward another enemy (stick within 35°, 3-12 m) mid-string = leap to it
  (zip-like), string kept, style bonus.

### Batch 7 (E17-18, B8)
- Star Rush: style fills a star meter (HUD star), full + R3 = 10 s: super armour (hits cost half,
  no knockback), +15% speed, star sparks, invincibility music at 0.06; no stacking with Mega.
- Stopwatch (spec E18): investigate freezing an enemy (anim speed / time_act), fallback design.
- Boss ride: hook a big one + hold RT: steer it (its root nudged toward stick dir) for up to 4 s,
  wall hit = open (swing stance broken); cd 40 s.

### Final
- Balance pass over all of G, README, release, memory, summary.
